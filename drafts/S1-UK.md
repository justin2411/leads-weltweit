## Vorlage 2: mehrere Stellen gleichzeitig
Subject: {region} companies hiring for several roles

Hello {agency} team,

Your focus on {specialism} roles in {region} caught my eye.

We watch the careers pages of local employers and note when a company is advertising three or more roles at the same time, or when a role reappears after being taken down. We send you the company, the roles, the source and the date we saw them.

It is a simple way to know which firms are busy hiring before you pick up the phone.

Could I send you a free sample of 10 leads from {region} to judge for yourself?

Kind regards,
{sender}
