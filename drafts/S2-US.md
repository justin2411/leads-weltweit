# Entwurfsvorlagen S2 (Webagenturen), US, Experiment-Botschaft v1

Vorlagen, noch ohne echte Empfänger. `{…}` wird pro Agentur individuell ersetzt.
Versand erst, wenn es echte Probe-Leads für den Bundesstaat bzw. die Stadt gibt.
Erste Quelle ist das Register von New York (data.ny.gov). Deshalb zuerst Agenturen im Großraum New York anschreiben: Käufer brauchen Leads aus ihrem eigenen Markt.

---
## Vorlage 1: Neugründungen ohne Website
Subject: New businesses in {city} without a website

Hello {agency} team,

I saw that {agency} builds websites for {niche} businesses in {city}.

We track new company registrations from public state records and check whether each business already has a website. New owners who have just registered a company, but have no site yet, are usually deciding right now who will build it.

Each lead includes the company name, registration date, the source and what we found online.

Would a free sample of 10 such leads from {city} be useful to you, with no obligation?

Best regards,
{sender}

Your work for {niche} clients around {city} caught my eye.

Could I send you 10 free sample leads from {city} to see if they fit?
