# Entwurfsvorlagen S9 (Finanzberater), UK, Experiment-Botschaft v1

Vorlagen, noch ohne echte Empfänger. `{…}` wird pro Firma individuell ersetzt.
Nur allgemeine Adressen (info@, hello@ …), nur Ltd/LLP (countries.yaml). Versand erst nach echten Probe-Leads und Freigabe.

## Vorlage 1: wachsende Arbeitgeber
Subject: Growing employers in {region}

I saw that {firm} advises business owners and employers around {region}.

We track local companies that are hiring for several roles at once or have recently opened a new site. Growing headcount usually brings questions about workplace pensions, employee benefits and protecting the business, which can be a good reason for a first conversation.

Each lead names the company, what we saw, where we found it and when.

Would a free sample of 10 such leads from {region} be useful to you, with no obligation?

Best regards,
{sender}
